🐛 Wormhole Simulation — How to Read This

What You're Looking At

This simulates a wormhole throat — a tunnel connecting two regions of spacetime.

The Four Energy Conditions (Red Bars)

These are always red for wormholes, and that's OK!

  • Normal matter satisfies these conditions
  • Wormholes require exotic matter (negative energy density)
  • This is a proven requirement — you can't have a traversable wormhole without it

The STABLE Badge

This is different from energy conditions!

  • STABLE (green) = The throat bounces back when pushed (like a spring)
  • UNSTABLE = The throat collapses or flies apart when pushed

Parameters Explained

MMass of the black hole seed
AString cloud strength (matter around the throat)
r₀Regulator scale (how "smeared out" the singularity is)
a₀Throat radius — where the wormhole is narrowest

EOS Models

The Equation of State describes what kind of matter fills the throat:

  • Phantom (default) — Can be stable with right parameters
  • Chaplygin — Cosmological model, may have stable branches
  • Barotropic — Simplest model, usually unstable for this geometry

Troubleshooting

  • If UNSTABLE: Try increasing a₀ or switching EOS models
  • If simulation runs too fast: Lower the speed multiplier
  • If page looks broken: Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R)
